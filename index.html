The balanced accuracy p-value of 0.040 confirms that the sPLS-DA multi-omics model achieves statistically significant group discrimination beyond chance, despite the small sample size (n=28). The overall accuracy p-value (0.072) does not reach the conventional threshold — consistent with the imbalanced group sizes giving more weight to the larger PE and PTD groups in overall accuracy. Balanced accuracy is therefore the primary reported metric.

Key Findings

Multi-omics integration outperforms single-modality classification

Arm C The combined metabolomics + transcriptomics model (Scenario A) reached 53.0% balanced accuracy — significantly above chance (p = 0.040, permutation test). Gene-only (38.4%) and metabolite-only (41.4%) models were substantially weaker, confirming that the two modalities carry complementary discriminatory information.

PE and PTD are not well-separated from each other

Arm C Binary classification (Control vs Disease) reached 64.3% accuracy — far higher than 3-class performance. This suggests the molecular signatures of PE and PTD share common ground, and that the 3-class problem is the harder and more informative target.

Sphingolipid / apoptosis axis — converging evidence from two arms

Arm BArm C D-Sphingosine and Sphingosine (d18:1) show 100% ML stability and PE-elevated Log2FC (~+0.97). Independently, TUBA3D correlates with both sphingosines and shares the Apoptosis pathway (map04210). Two separate analytical arms point to the same biological axis.

PTD has a stronger univariate gene signal

Arm A 2.7× more genes reach nominal significance in PTD vs Control (294) than in PE vs Control (111). Top PTD-stable genes include DFFA (apoptosis), FNDC10, SNORA44, and several RN7SL RNA genes. No genes survived FDR correction in either comparison — consistent with n=28 power limits.

Limitations

Sample size constrains statistical power

n=28 is insufficient for genome-wide FDR correction across 315,576 correlation pairs or 4,383 differential expression tests. All results are exploratory and require validation in an independent cohort. The permutation test (p=0.040) confirms the ML model is non-random, but not its clinical utility.

Hyperparameter tuning on full dataset

keepX for sPLS-DA was tuned on all 28 samples before LOOCV prediction. This is an accepted approximation for n<30, but introduces a mild optimistic bias in the hyperparameter selection step. Accuracy and p-values are not affected (they come from the prediction loop only).

Pooled Spearman correlation

Gene–metabolite Spearman correlations were computed across all 28 samples pooled, not within groups. A correlation may reflect biological co-regulation or group composition effects. Within-group correlations would require n≥30 per group.
